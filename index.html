About Us

With a wide variety of hi-tech industrial tools of the most reliable make, Hi-Tech Tools is a leading manufacturer and supplier of Progressive Electrical Stamping Dies & Tools, Press Tools, Sheet Metal Dies & Tools. We are also a trusted service provider, offering job work for CNC Wire-Cut.

Led by our promoter Mr. Bhupendra Sharma, we are known for providing quality, reliability and timely services. We work with the sole motto of "Better Quality, Maximum Economy and Prompt Delivery."
